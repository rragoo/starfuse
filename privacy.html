Privacy policy

Starfuse · last updated November 18, 2026

Starfuse is a puzzle game you can play in a web browser and in the iPhone, iPad and Android apps. This page explains what the game collects, why, who else sees it, and how to have it removed. Questions? Write to starfuse@example.com.

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your email, real name, contacts, location or photos.
  • The game sends anonymous play stats. We store no ID, name or IP address with them.
  • If you join the daily leaderboard, the nickname you pick and your score are public.
  • The apps show optional ads from Google AdMob. On iPhone and iPad you decide whether apps may track you.
  • We don’t sell your data.

What stays on your device

Your saved game (coins, best scores, themes, collections and settings) is kept in your browser’s or the app’s own storage. The apps also keep a backup copy in the app’s settings storage, so the save survives if the phone clears the game’s web storage. None of this is sent to us. We can’t see it or restore it. Deleting the app, or clearing the site’s data in your browser, removes it.

Anonymous play stats

To see how many people play, how many come back, and whether the game is balanced, the game sends a few anonymous events to our database:

  • Once a day per device: days since this device first played and since it last played; the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer) and operating system; whether it’s the app; language and time zone; the website that linked to the game; coins and power-ups saved up; and which half of a test group the device is in.
  • For each run: the mode, score, number of drops, length, biggest planet, theme, screen frame rate, power-ups used and coins earned.
  • Some actions: sharing a score, buying with coins, ad rewards, claiming rewards, trying a theme, and opening a menu (once a day each).

These events carry no ID, name or account, so they can’t be tied to you or to each other. Like any web request, each one reaches the server with your IP address, but the address isn’t saved with the stats. Browsers that send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control send no stats at all. The stats are kept in our database at Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany, for as long as Starfuse runs.

The daily leaderboard

Joining the daily leaderboard is optional. If you add a nickname, the game posts your best daily score with:

  • the nickname you chose, your score, your biggest planet, a star if you have made a supernova, and the date. All of these are public: everyone playing Starfuse can see them;
  • a random player ID that the game creates on your device, so the board keeps one row per player.

Nicknames are filtered automatically. Please don’t use your real name or anything that identifies you. If you report someone’s name (the flag next to it), we store the report with your random player ID so each person can report a name only once. Past days’ leaderboards are kept. To have your entries removed, email us with your nickname and the days you played.

Ads in the apps

The iPhone, iPad and Android apps show ads from Google AdMob, and only when you choose to watch one: to keep a run going, double your coins, or get a free power-up. The website shows no ads. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google collects information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device details and how you interact with ads. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

  • On iPhone and iPad, Apple asks once whether Starfuse may track you. If you say no, Google doesn’t get your device’s advertising identifier. You can change your answer in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choice after your first run. Change it any time with “Ad privacy” on the game’s menu.
  • To report an ad that looked wrong, use Help & privacy › Report an ad in the app, or email us.

Google explains its use of this data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing your score

The share picture is made on your device and goes only where you send it. The apps ask for access to Photos only to save that picture when you choose Save Image.

The website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub receives your IP address to deliver the pages (GitHub’s privacy statement). Its fonts come from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for them. The apps carry their fonts with them.

Who else handles data

Only the services named above: Supabase (our database), Google (AdMob ads in the apps, fonts on the website) and GitHub (website hosting). Each is bound by its own privacy and data-processing terms to protect this data at least as well as this policy describes, and they use it only to provide their service to Starfuse.

Children

Starfuse is made for a general audience. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. The nickname is optional, and the ads in the apps are limited to general-audience content. If you think a child has posted something identifying on the leaderboard, email us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • Stop the play stats on the website by turning on Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control in your browser.
  • Change your ad choices with “Ad privacy” (EU, UK, Switzerland) or in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Delete your saved game by deleting the app or clearing the site’s data.
  • Have your leaderboard entries removed, or ask anything about your data: starfuse@example.com.

Because the play stats carry no ID, we can’t find or delete one person’s stats.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. If the change is significant, the game will say so.
