Support

Drop planets into a jar. Two of the same planet fuse into the next one, from Stardust up to the Sun.

Questions, bugs, a name on the ranks that shouldn’t be there, or an ad that looked wrong: write to support@starfuse.app. We read every message.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, and Android phones and tablets. Starfuse is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

How do I play?

Slide your finger to aim and lift it to drop the planet. Two matching planets fuse into a bigger one. The run ends when a planet stays above the danger line near the top for 3 seconds. “How to play” on the menu shows it all again.

My progress is gone

Your saved game lives on your device only. Deleting the app removes it, and there’s no online copy to restore yet.

How do I change my leaderboard name?

Open Ranks and tap “Change” under the list. Names that break the rules are refused.

Someone’s name is offensive

Tap the flag next to it on the Ranks screen. The name is hidden for you straight away, and we review every report and remove names that break the rules. You can also email us.

An ad looked wrong

Open Help & privacy on the game’s menu and tap “Report an ad”, or email us with when you saw it. Ads only play when you choose to watch one.

Ad and tracking choices

On iPhone and iPad: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, “Ad privacy” on the game’s menu changes your ad consent.

Purchases

Starfuse doesn’t sell anything for real money yet. Coins, themes and power-ups are all earned by playing.
