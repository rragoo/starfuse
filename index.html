Starfuse is a physics puzzle for your phone. Drop planets into a jar, match two to fuse them into the next one, and climb from Stardust all the way to the Sun.

How it plays

Easy to start. Hard to put down.

Every run is a race against the pile. Fuse planets to make room, chain fusions for combos, and see how big you can grow before the jar fills up.

Drop

Slide to aim and lift to let go. Each planet falls, rolls and settles into the pile.

Fuse

Two of the same planet merge into the next one up. One drop can set off a whole chain of fusions.

Keep it under the line

If a planet rests above the danger line for 3 seconds, the run is over. Then it’s one more go.

Eleven worlds, from Stardust to the Sun

Every fusion makes something bigger. Reach the Sun, then make two Suns collide: they go supernova and open a new galaxy, with more points to win.

What’s inside

Something new every day

Daily challenge

The same planets for everyone, every day. Post your best score and see where you rank worldwide.

Pop, Swap, Shake

Pop the planet in the way, swap the one in your hand, or shake the jar to knock loose new matches.

Nine themes

Cosmos, Candy Orbit, Squishies, Plushies, Animal Pals, Axolotls, Pixel Mine, Fruit Basket and Squeeze Balls.

Supernovas

Two Suns collide into a supernova and open the next galaxy, where every fusion is worth more.

Missions and rewards

Three new missions a day, a daily reward calendar, and a Star Pass full of coins and power-ups.

Made to feel good

Squishy landings, a little buzz on every fusion, and music that builds as the jar fills up.
